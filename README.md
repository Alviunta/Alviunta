## Hi, I'm Alvaro Giunta

Electronics engineer (UTN) and co-founder of [MateWare Electronics Studio](https://mateware.com.ar/en/), an electronics engineering studio based in Mendoza, Argentina.

### About MateWare

We design custom electronic products end to end: hardware, firmware and industrial design, from the first idea to series production. Our clients work with a single technical partner across the whole development cycle.

We also build our own product, [MoTI](https://mateware.com.ar/en/moti/), an industrial monitoring and telemetry platform (IIoT).

### What I do

At MateWare I split my time between engineering and running the company, where I lead administration and finance.

On the technical side I focus on:

- **Embedded firmware:** C on STM32 microcontrollers (H7 series), bare-metal and RTOS-based
- **Hardware design:** schematics and PCB layout in KiCad, bring-up and hardware-in-the-loop testing
- **Wireless and IoT:** LoRa/LoRaWAN devices and data pipelines
- **Technical documentation:** LaTeX-based document systems with versioning
- **Tooling:** Python scripts to automate testing, documentation and internal processes
